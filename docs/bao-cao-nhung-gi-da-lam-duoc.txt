BAO CAO NHUNG NOI DUNG DA THUC HIEN TRONG DO AN
De tai: Xay dung he thong Chatbot da kenh tich hop CRM cho ban hang online
Ten he thong: Smart Merchant Hub

======================================================================
1. TONG QUAN HE THONG
======================================================================

Smart Merchant Hub la nen tang CRM ket hop Chatbot AI/RAG, ho tro doanh
nghiep quan ly hoi thoai khach hang tu nhieu kenh tap trung tren mot he
thong.

He thong duoc xay dung theo mo hinh:

- Frontend: Vue.js.
- Backend: FastAPI/Python.
- Co so du lieu: PostgreSQL.
- Tim kiem vector: pgvector.
- Dong goi va trien khai: Docker Compose.
- Mo hinh AI: ho tro Groq, Gemini va OpenAI cho phan sinh cau tra loi.
- Embedding: ho tro local Sentence-Transformers, Gemini va OpenAI.
- Xu ly tai lieu: PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, Markdown va HTML.

======================================================================
2. CAC CHUC NANG DA THUC HIEN
======================================================================

2.1. Khoi dong va quan ly he thong

- Tao FastAPI backend voi cac endpoint root va health check.
- Cau hinh CORS de frontend co the goi API backend.
- Khoi tao co so du lieu tu dong khi backend khoi dong.
- Kich hoat pgvector neu PostgreSQL chua co extension vector.
- Ho tro chay rieng backend/frontend va chay dong bo bang Docker Compose.

2.2. CRM khach hang va hoi thoai

- Luu thong tin khach hang theo kenh giao tiep.
- Luu lich su hoi thoai cua tung khach hang.
- Luu tin nhan inbound va outbound.
- Ho tro noi dung van ban, hinh anh, video va tep dinh kem.
- Luu external_user_id va external_message_id de doi chieu voi nen tang
  ben ngoai.
- Chong luu trung tin nhan bang external_message_id.
- Ho tro trang thai hoi thoai, do uu tien, nguoi phu trach va lich su
  phan cong trong mo hinh du lieu.
- Ho tro cap nhat hoi thoai theo thoi gian thuc qua WebSocket.

2.3. Tich hop cac kenh ben ngoai

- Da tao endpoint webhook cho Facebook.
- Da tao endpoint webhook cho Instagram.
- Da tao endpoint webhook cho Shopee.
- Da tao endpoint webhook cho TikTok.
- Da xay dung lop chuan hoa du lieu tin nhan ve mo hinh chung.
- Da xu ly cac truong hop tin nhan van ban, media, attachment va su kien
  webhook khong phai tin nhan.
- Da co co che gui tin nhan va hinh anh ra Facebook/Instagram.

2.4. Meta OAuth va quan ly ket noi

- Da tao luong OAuth cho Facebook Page.
- Da lay thong tin Facebook Page va tai khoan Instagram lien ket.
- Da dang ky webhook messages cho Facebook Page sau khi OAuth thanh cong.
- Da luu token, thong tin Page, thong tin Instagram va trang thai ket noi
  vao bang app_settings.
- Da co chuc nang xem trang thai ket noi va ngat ket noi tren giao dien.

2.5. Quan ly kho tri thuc

- Cho phep upload tai lieu tu giao dien.
- Kiem tra dinh dang file va gioi han dung luong.
- Tao ban ghi tai lieu voi cac trang thai pending, processing, ready va
  error.
- Doc noi dung tai lieu.
- Chia tai lieu thanh cac doan text chunk.
- Tao embedding cho cac chunk.
- Luu noi dung, metadata va vector vao PostgreSQL/pgvector.
- Cho phep xem danh sach tai lieu.
- Cho phep xem chi tiet chunk cua tai lieu.
- Cho phep xoa tai lieu va cac chunk lien quan.
- Ho tro tu dong nap bo du lieu mau khi backend khoi dong.

2.6. Chatbot RAG

- Da trien khai API chat khong streaming.
- Da trien khai API chat streaming qua Server-Sent Events.
- Da tim kiem ket hop semantic retrieval va lexical retrieval.
- Da lay top-k chunk lien quan tu kho tri thuc.
- Da xay dung prompt tu cau hoi va context tim duoc.
- Da goi mo hinh LLM de tao cau tra loi.
- Da tra ve cac nguon tai lieu tham khao kem cau tra loi.
- Da ghi log qua trinh RAG vao file rag_runs.jsonl.

2.7. Tu dong tra loi

- Da co cau hinh bat/tat auto-reply.
- Khi co tin nhan inbound, he thong co the truy van kho tri thuc.
- Neu tim thay context phu hop, he thong tao cau tra loi bang LLM.
- He thong gui cau tra loi ra Facebook/Instagram.
- Cau tra loi outbound duoc luu lai vao lich su tin nhan.

2.8. Giao dien nguoi dung

- Da co giao dien quan ly hoi thoai tap trung.
- Da co danh sach khach hang va hoi thoai.
- Da co giao dien gui tin nhan van ban.
- Da co giao dien upload va quan ly tai lieu.
- Da co giao dien RAG AI Assistant.
- Da co chuc nang bat/tat auto-reply.
- Da co giao dien cau hinh ket noi Facebook/Instagram.
- Da co hien thi trang thai tai lieu: cho xu ly, dang xu ly, san sang va
  loi.

3. CO SO DU LIEU DA THIET KE
======================================================================

He thong hien co mo hinh muc tieu 20 bang. Trong do app_settings la bang
cau hinh da co tu phien ban single-shop; cac bang con lai duoc mo rong de
phu hop voi mo hinh nhieu doanh nghiep trong tai lieu do an.

Danh sach 20 bang:

01. app_settings              - Cau hinh he thong, Meta OAuth, auto-reply.
02. businesses                - Thong tin doanh nghiep.
03. users                     - Tai khoan va nhan vien.
04. service_plans             - Cac goi dich vu.
05. subscriptions             - Goi dich vu doanh nghiep dang su dung.
06. payments                  - Giao dich dang ky/gia han.
07. channels                  - Kenh Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Shopee.
08. channel_events            - Su kien webhook va chong trung event.
09. customers                 - Ho so khach hang cuoi.
10. conversations             - Hoi thoai tap trung.
11. conversation_assignments  - Lich su phan cong hoi thoai.
12. messages                  - Tin nhan inbound/outbound.
13. tags                      - Nhan nghiep vu.
14. conversation_tags         - Quan he hoi thoai va nhan.
15. products                  - San pham duoc tu van.
16. orders                    - Don hang.
17. order_items               - Chi tiet don hang.
18. documents                 - Tai lieu kho tri thuc.
19. document_chunks           - Chunk va vector embedding.
20. chatbot_configs           - Cau hinh Chatbot theo doanh nghiep.

Da bo sung cac model SQLAlchemy tai:

- backend/app/models/business.py
- backend/app/models/channel.py
- backend/app/models/crm_extended.py
- backend/app/models/sales.py
- backend/app/models/chatbot.py

Da mo rong cac model cu:

- customer.py: them business_id, email, phone, address va updated_at.
- conversation.py: them business_id, channel_id, priority, assigned_user_id,
  last_message_at va closed_at.
- message.py: them sender_type, sender_user_id, status, metadata va sent_at.
- document.py: them business_id de tach kho tri thuc theo doanh nghiep.

Da cap nhat init_db.py de:

- Tu dong tao cac bang moi.
- Them cac cot moi cho database cu bang migration idempotent.
- Cap nhat rang buoc unique khach hang theo doanh nghiep va kenh.
- Van giu cac cot tenant nullable trong giai doan chuyen doi de khong lam
  hong du lieu single-shop hien tai.

8. NHUNG NOI DUNG CHUA HOAN THIEN
======================================================================

De bao cao trung thuc, cac noi dung sau moi dung o muc mo hinh/model hoac
khung thiet ke, chua co API nghiep vu day du:

- Dang nhap JWT va phan quyen nguoi dung chua hoan thien.
- business_id chua duoc lay tu session/JWT trong moi request.
- OAuth Meta hien van luu cau hinh tuong thich trong app_settings; bang
  channels da co nhung chua duoc noi day du vao luong OAuth.
- channel_events da duoc thiet ke nhung webhook hien tai chua ghi moi event
  vao bang nay trong tat ca cac kenh.
- API CRUD cho businesses, users, products, orders, tags, subscriptions va
  payments chua duoc xay dung day du.
- Bao cao thong ke chua co man hinh dashboard nghiep vu day du.
- RAG run log hien dang luu trong file rag_runs.jsonl, chua dua vao mot bang
  database rieng.
- Can tiep tuc kiem thu end-to-end voi PostgreSQL/pgvector va cac API that
  cua Facebook, Instagram, Shopee va TikTok.
- Mo hinh Sentence-Transformers can tai model lan dau; thoi gian xu ly file
  products.csv lon phu thuoc vao CPU/RAM.
