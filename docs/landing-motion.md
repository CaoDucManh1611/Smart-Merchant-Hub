# Landing page visuals

Platform glyphs are local vector assets from Simple Icons v16 (CC0 project):
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@v16/icons/
Platform trademarks remain owned by their respective owners; no partnership is implied.

Three bilingual introductions rotate every seven seconds while visible.
Automatic rotation stops on keyboard focus or hidden tabs, but not on hover or manual selection,
reduced-motion preference, or the global motion control. All timers and observers
are cleaned up on unmount. Product and customer panels are labeled illustrations.
