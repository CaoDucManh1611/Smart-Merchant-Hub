## P1 fixes and code areas

- **Appointment:** `backend/app/api/appointments.py` rejects status, schedule, customer/service, assignee, and reminder edits without `APPOINTMENT_TOOLS`; it permits only notes-only edits and does not enqueue reminders for those edits. The route-level capability guard continues to allow safe history reads.
- **Catalog authorization:** `backend/app/api/chat.py` now authorizes before `answer_from_crm`, RAG retrieval, or live product context in both normal and streaming chat. `backend/app/services/conversation_graph.py` guards early product-identity probes. `backend/app/services/auto_reply_service.py` avoids Product lookup during SKU formatting unless authorized. Direct recommendation operations have backend guards in `backend/app/api/recommendations.py`.
- **Payment:** `backend/app/tenancy/workspace_modules.py` reports the dependency available only when the tenant has enabled, complete bank-transfer configuration and the encrypted account can be decrypted and validated. `backend/app/services/capability_registry.py` requires that dependency. Missing/corrupt configuration fails closed.
